Treasure trips

Fly to a planet, meet an alien, and collect as many space coins as you can.

  1. 1Every trip has two steps. First, choose one of two rockets.
  2. 2Each rocket usually flies to the same planet, but now and then it ends up at the other one.
  3. 3On the planet, choose one of two aliens. An alien might give you a space coin, or nothing.
  4. 4How likely each alien is to give a coin changes slowly over time, so keep watching which ones are doing well now.
  5. 5There's no timer, so take your time. The game ends by itself when it's over.
